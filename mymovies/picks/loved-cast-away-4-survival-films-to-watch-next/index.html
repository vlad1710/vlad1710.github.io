Loved Cast Away? 4 survival films to watch next

If Cast Away stayed with you long after Wilson floated away, here are four survival films with the same core: one person against nature, impossible odds and the stubborn will to get home. Spoiler-free, ready for your watchlist.

  1. The Revenant (2015)

    Brutal frontier survival, shot in real freezing wilderness

    A fur trapper left for dead fights his way back across a frozen frontier. Shot in natural light in harsh real locations, it feels as physical as Cast Away, just colder and angrier.

  2. 127 Hours (2010)

    One man, one canyon, and a fight to get out

    A climber gets trapped alone in a remote Utah canyon. Danny Boyle turns a single location into a tense, surprisingly energetic story about will and regret — perfect if Chuck's solitude stuck with you.

  3. Gravity (2013)

    Stranded in orbit — 90 minutes of pure tension

    An astronaut is cut loose after debris destroys her shuttle. It's a lean, breathless survival story that works best on the biggest screen you have, and it's barely 90 minutes.

  4. All Is Lost (2013)

    Robert Redford alone at sea, almost no dialogue

    A lone sailor's yacht is damaged in the middle of the Indian Ocean. Almost wordless and quietly gripping, it's the closest spiritual cousin to Cast Away's island chapters.
