Loved Gone Girl? Watch these next

Gone Girl left you suspicious of everyone? These four thrillers bring the same dark mysteries, cold-blooded tension and twists that keep you guessing — three of them from the same director, David Fincher.

  1. Prisoners (2013)

    A desperate father, a missing child — tension that never lets go

    Denis Villeneuve's slow-burn thriller keeps you guessing until the very end. Like Gone Girl, it's a dark mystery where every character might be hiding something.

  2. Zodiac (2007)

    Fincher again — an obsession with a case that won't stay solved

    Same director as Gone Girl, the same cold precision. A gripping, detail-packed hunt for a real-life killer that turns into a story about obsession.

  3. Shutter Island (2010)

    Nothing on this island is what it seems

    A moody, atmospheric mystery with Leonardo DiCaprio as a detective on a remote asylum island. If you loved Gone Girl's twists, this one plays with your head too.

  4. Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011)

    A cold case, a hacker and a family full of secrets

    Another Fincher thriller (The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo): a journalist and a brilliant hacker dig into a decades-old disappearance. Stylish, tense and very hard to stop watching.
