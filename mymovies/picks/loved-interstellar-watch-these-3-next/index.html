Loved Interstellar? Watch these 3 next

Interstellar left you wanting more space, more science and more feelings? These three films hit the same notes — big questions about the universe, told through very human stories. All spoiler-free.

  1. Arrival (2016)

    Quiet, emotional sci-fi that rewires how you think about time

    A linguist is brought in when mysterious ships land around the world. Like Interstellar, it's big-idea science fiction anchored in a deeply personal story — and the final act lands hard.

  2. Contact (1997)

    A scientist, a signal from space and a question of faith

    Jodie Foster plays an astronomer who picks up what might be a message from another world. It shares Interstellar's mix of real science, wonder and a parent-child heart.

  3. The Martian (2015)

    Stranded on Mars, solving it with science — and humor

    An astronaut left behind on Mars has to science his way to survival. Same space-survival tension as Interstellar, but lighter, funnier and endlessly rewatchable.
