Loved Parasite? Watch these 3 next

Parasite opened a lot of eyes to Korean cinema. If you want more of its sharp mix of suspense, dark humor and class tension, these three films are the perfect next step — spoiler-free picks to add to your watchlist.

  1. Memories of Murder (2003)

    Bong Joon-ho's gripping detective story — funny, then chilling

    Before Parasite, Bong Joon-ho made this tense, darkly funny crime drama about two detectives in over their heads in 1980s rural Korea. The same mix of humor and dread, and an ending you'll keep thinking about.

  2. The Handmaiden (2016)

    A twisty con-artist thriller where nobody is who they seem

    Park Chan-wook's lush, sharply plotted thriller set in 1930s Korea. If you loved how Parasite kept shifting under your feet, this one does it in three acts, each flipping what you thought you knew.

  3. Burning (2018)

    A slow-burn mystery about class and envy that gets under your skin

    Lee Chang-dong's quiet, hypnotic film about a young man, an old friend and a rich stranger with a strange hobby. It shares Parasite's eye for class tension, but lets the suspense simmer instead of explode.
