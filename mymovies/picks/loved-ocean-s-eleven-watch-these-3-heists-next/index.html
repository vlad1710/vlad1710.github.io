Loved Ocean's Eleven? Watch these 3 heists next

Loved the crew, the plan and the last-minute twist of Ocean's Eleven? These three heist movies deliver the same thrill — from a cryptic bank robbery to a scrappy race-day job by the very same director. No spoilers.

  1. Inside Man (2006)

    A bank heist where the robber seems to be one step ahead of everyone

    Spike Lee's slick thriller with Denzel Washington as the negotiator and Clive Owen as the calm, cryptic robber. Like Ocean's Eleven, the fun is in watching a plan you don't fully understand until the very end.

  2. The Town (2010)

    Tense Boston robberies with real stakes and a crew you'll care about

    Ben Affleck directs and stars as a career thief who starts to want out. Grittier than Ocean's, but with the same pleasure of a tight crew and beautifully staged heists.

  3. Logan Lucky (2017)

    Steven Soderbergh's other heist — Ocean's, but in a NASCAR town

    The director of Ocean's Eleven returns to the genre with a scrappy, funny crew pulling a job during a big race. Light, clever and full of small surprises — perfect if you loved the playful tone.
