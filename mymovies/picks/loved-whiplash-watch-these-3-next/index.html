Loved Whiplash? Watch these 3 next

Whiplash is about obsession, pressure and how far someone will go to be great. If that intensity stuck with you, these three films hit the same nerve — from the ballet stage to the LA night to a drummer's sudden silence. No spoilers.

  1. Black Swan (2010)

    Perfection at any cost — on a ballet stage

    A ballerina chasing the role of her life starts to lose her grip. Same suffocating pressure as Whiplash, but darker and dreamlike. Natalie Portman is unforgettable.

  2. Nightcrawler (2014)

    Ambition turns ugly in the LA night

    A driven loner breaks into crime news and won't stop climbing. Jake Gyllenhaal is chilling, and the tension never lets up — pure Whiplash energy without the drums.

  3. Sound of Metal (2020)

    A drummer's world goes silent overnight

    A metal drummer suddenly starts losing his hearing. Raw, intimate and with some of the best sound design ever — a must for anyone who felt every beat in Whiplash.
