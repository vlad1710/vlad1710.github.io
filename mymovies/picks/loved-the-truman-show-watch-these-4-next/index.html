Loved The Truman Show? Watch these 4 next

The Truman Show is a funny, warm film about a man who starts to suspect his life isn't what it seems. These four films play with reality, identity and being watched in the same playful way. Two of them star Jim Carrey's dramatic side, and all are spoiler-free picks for your next movie night.

  1. Pleasantville (1998)

    Two teens get trapped in a perfect black-and-white TV town

    Like Truman's Seahaven, Pleasantville looks like a flawless sitcom world — until someone starts asking questions. A warm, clever fable about what we lose when life is too 'perfect'.

  2. Stranger Than Fiction (2006)

    A man hears a voice narrating his life — and it's not good news

    Will Ferrell plays a tax auditor who discovers he's a character in someone's novel. Funny, gentle and surprisingly moving — the same mix of comedy and existential worry that makes Truman work.

  3. Being John Malkovich (1999)

    A hidden door leads straight into a famous actor's head

    A strange, inventive comedy about identity and being watched from the inside. If you liked how Truman plays with reality and performance, this one takes the idea somewhere wild.

  4. Eternal Sunshine (2004)

    Erasing a breakup from your memory? It's never that simple

    Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind stars Jim Carrey again in a dramatic role. A bittersweet, inventive love story about memory — perfect if you loved Carrey's softer side in Truman.
