Thanksgiving movie night? Watch these 3

Thanksgiving week means family, food and the inevitable "what should we watch?" debate. These three comedies are all about getting home for the holidays — and surviving it — so they're easy to agree on and fun to rewatch every year.

  1. Planes, Trains and Automobiles (1987)

    The ultimate trying-to-get-home-for-Thanksgiving comedy

    Steve Martin and John Candy as the world's most mismatched travel buddies, racing to get home before the holiday. Big laughs that turn surprisingly warm by the end — a Thanksgiving tradition for a reason.

  2. Little Miss Sunshine (2006)

    A chaotic family road trip that's funny and big-hearted

    A wonderfully dysfunctional family crams into a yellow VW bus for a road trip across the country. Sharp, awkward, hilarious and touching — perfect when the whole family is in one room.

  3. Home for the Holidays (1995)

    Holiday dinner chaos every family will recognize

    Jodie Foster's comedy about flying home for one very messy Thanksgiving dinner with parents and siblings. Holly Hunter and Robert Downey Jr. make the family arguments feel painfully, lovingly real.
