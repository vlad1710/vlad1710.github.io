Waiting for Dune: Part Three? Watch these 3

Dune: Part Three is on the way. If you want to stay in that mood of huge deserts, prophecies and slow-burning epic storytelling, these three films are the perfect warm-up: one from Villeneuve himself, one classic that shaped the genre and one animated saga fans love to compare with Herbert's world.

  1. Blade Runner 2049 (2017)

    Villeneuve's other giant: vast, hypnotic, unforgettable

    Before Dune, Denis Villeneuve made this slow-burning sci-fi epic. The same huge visuals, the same booming sound and the same quiet, lonely hero searching for the truth. Best on the biggest screen you have.

  2. Lawrence of Arabia (1962)

    The desert epic that every sand saga still borrows from

    An outsider in the desert, a people who follow him, and the danger of becoming a legend. If Paul's story grabbed you, this classic feels like its ancestor. Long, but every frame is a painting.

  3. Nausicaä (1984)

    Miyazaki's eco-saga with giant creatures and a chosen hero

    Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind is a post-apocalyptic adventure about a young princess, a poisoned world and enormous beasts nobody understands. Fans often compare its ecology and prophecy themes to Dune.
