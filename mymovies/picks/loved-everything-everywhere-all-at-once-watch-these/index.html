Loved Everything Everywhere All at Once? Watch these

Everything Everywhere All at Once mixed absurd humour, action and real emotion like few films before it. If you want more of that energy, these three films are just as inventive, funny and unexpectedly moving.

  1. Swiss Army Man (2016)

    Same directors, same glorious weirdness — and a big heart

    The Daniels' first feature: a stranded man and a very unusual companion. It's absurd, often hilarious, and surprisingly tender, which is exactly the mix that made Everything Everywhere work.

  2. Sorry to Bother You (2018)

    A sharp comedy that swerves somewhere you'll never predict

    A telemarketer discovers a trick that sends his career soaring, and things get stranger with every scene. Bold, funny and full of ideas, it has the same fearless energy as Everything Everywhere.

  3. Scott Pilgrim vs. the World (2010)

    Video-game fights, big feelings, nonstop visual invention

    To date the girl of his dreams, Scott has to defeat her seven evil exes. Edgar Wright's kinetic editing and comic-book style will scratch the same itch as Evelyn's kung-fu multiverse battles.
