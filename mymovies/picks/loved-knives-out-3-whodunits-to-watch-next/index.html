Loved Knives Out? 3 whodunits to watch next

If you loved the twists, the big cast and the sharp humor of Knives Out, these three whodunits deliver the same pleasure: a closed circle of suspects, a clever investigation and a reveal you'll want to talk about. No spoilers below.

  1. Clue (1985)

    A board game turned cult comedy — with three different endings

    Six guests, one mansion, one dead host and a butler who knows too much. It's fast, silly and sharp — the movie Knives Out fans quote without realizing it. Famous for its multiple endings.

  2. Gosford Park (2001)

    Upstairs, downstairs, and everyone has a motive

    A 1930s country-house weekend where the servants see everything. Robert Altman's ensemble mystery is as much about class as about who did it — a slow-burn treat for detail lovers.

  3. See How They Run (2022)

    A cozy, self-aware murder mystery in 1950s London

    A clumsy inspector and an eager rookie investigate a murder backstage at a hit West End play. Light, witty and full of nods to the genre — perfect for a November evening.
