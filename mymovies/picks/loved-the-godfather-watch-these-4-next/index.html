Loved The Godfather? Watch these 4 next

If The Godfather left you wanting more family loyalty, power and slow-burning tension, these four crime epics deliver the same weight — each in its own style, from Scorsese's energy to Michael Mann's cool precision.

  1. Goodfellas (1990)

    Mob life from the inside — fast, funny, then terrifying

    Scorsese tells a gangster story from the point of view of a guy who always wanted in. Energetic narration, a legendary soundtrack and performances that made it a genre benchmark.

  2. Heat (1995)

    Pacino vs De Niro in a cool, precise L.A. crime saga

    Michael Mann's epic about a professional crew and the detective chasing them. Long, patient and stylish, with one of the most famous shootouts in film history.

  3. Once Upon a Time in America (1984)

    Sergio Leone's sweeping, haunting gangster epic

    Friendship, ambition and memory across decades of New York. It's long and melancholic — perfect if what you loved in The Godfather was its sense of time and loss.

  4. The Departed (2006)

    Two moles, one city, nonstop tension

    A cop undercover in the mob and a mobster undercover in the police. Scorsese's Oscar winner is tight, twisty and packed with great performances.
