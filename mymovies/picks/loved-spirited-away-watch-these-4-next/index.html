Loved Spirited Away? Watch these 4 next

Spirited Away is the gateway for so many people into anime films. If you finished it and want that same mix of wonder and heart, these four films are a great next step — from gentle, emotional stories to a full-on dream trip.

  1. Your Name (2016)

    Two strangers, one impossible link — gorgeous and moving

    Makoto Shinkai's breakout film mixes everyday teen life with something much stranger. If you loved how Spirited Away makes the magical feel real, this one hits the same spot — and the animation is stunning.

  2. Wolf Children (2012)

    A warm, quiet fairy tale about raising two very unusual kids

    Mamoru Hosoda tells a gentle story about a young mother and her children who are not quite ordinary. Cozy, emotional and full of nature — perfect for a calm evening.

  3. A Silent Voice (2016)

    A tender story about guilt, kindness and second chances

    No magic here, but the same big heart. A grounded, honest film about school, regret and learning to reach out to people — one that stays with you for days.

  4. Paprika (2006)

    A wild ride through dreams — Inception-level trippy

    Satoshi Kon's colorful, surreal adventure is for the moments in Spirited Away when the world turned strange. Fast, inventive and unlike anything else.
