Can't agree on a movie? Try these 3

When one of you wants action and the other wants romance, these three films keep the whole couch happy. They mix adventure, humour and heart, so they're an easy yes for a couple or a group of friends.

  1. The Princess Bride (1987)

    Romance, sword fights and jokes — something for everyone

    A fairy tale that never takes itself too seriously: adventure, romance, giants and some of the most quotable lines ever. It works whether you came for the love story or the swashbuckling.

  2. Edge of Tomorrow (2014)

    Sci-fi action with real laughs and a sharp heroine

    A soldier stuck reliving the same day of battle — it's clever, fast and surprisingly funny. Action fans get spectacle, everyone else gets a great time-loop puzzle and two charismatic leads.

  3. Crazy, Stupid, Love. (2011)

    A rom-com that even rom-com skeptics end up enjoying

    Several tangled love stories, a great ensemble cast and a few genuinely surprising turns. Warm and funny without being sugary, so it's an easy yes for a mixed couch.
