Loved Alien? Watch these 3 next

If Alien is your kind of movie — isolation, a crew in trouble and something very wrong in the dark — these three space and sci-fi horror films deliver the same slow-building dread. No spoilers, just reasons to press play.

  1. Sunshine (2007)

    A crew flies to reignite the dying sun. The tension never lets go

    Danny Boyle's space thriller puts a small crew on a mission where one mistake ends everything. Like Alien, it's claustrophobic, beautifully shot and gets under your skin slowly before it really bites.

  2. Annihilation (2018)

    A strange zone where nature mutates. Gorgeous and deeply unsettling

    Alex Garland sends a team of scientists into an area where the rules of biology stop working. It's eerie rather than loud, with one scene people still talk about years later.

  3. Event Horizon (1997)

    A rescue ship finds a lost vessel that came back... wrong

    A cult favourite that mixes deep-space sci-fi with haunted-house horror. If the 'something is on the ship' feeling of Alien is what you loved, this one turns it up.
