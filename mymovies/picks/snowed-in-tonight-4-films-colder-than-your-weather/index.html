Snowed in tonight? 4 films colder than your weather

When it's cold outside, some films just hit harder. These four use snow, ice and isolation as part of the story — from a darkly funny Minnesota crime tale to a paranoid Antarctic thriller. Spoiler-free picks for your next winter movie night.

  1. Fargo (1996)

    A small-town crime gone wrong in endless Minnesota snow

    The Coen brothers turn a frozen Midwestern landscape into the backdrop for a darkly funny crime story. Frances McDormand's warm, unflappable police chief is the perfect contrast to the icy setting.

  2. The Thing (1982)

    Antarctic station, 12 men, and no one can trust anyone

    John Carpenter's isolated research base in Antarctica is one of the most claustrophobic settings in film. Paranoia builds with every scene, and the practical effects still hold up today.

  3. Wind River (2017)

    A quiet, gripping mystery on a snowbound reservation

    Taylor Sheridan's thriller follows a tracker and an FBI agent through the harsh Wyoming winter. Slow-burning, emotional and beautifully shot in deep snow.

  4. The Hateful Eight (2015)

    A blizzard traps eight strangers in one cabin

    Tarantino locks a group of shady characters inside a mountain lodge while a storm rages outside. Sharp dialogue, rising tension and a snowy Western mood make it a long but rewarding winter watch.
