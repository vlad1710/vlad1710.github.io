Seen Home Alone 20 times? Try these 3 this Christmas

Love the holidays but tired of watching the same Christmas classics every year? These four films are warm, funny and festive — and far less overplayed. Add them to your watchlist now so they're ready when December evenings arrive.

  1. Klaus (2019)

    A gorgeous animated origin story for Santa that feels brand new

    A selfish postman is exiled to a frozen island town and stumbles into something bigger. The hand-drawn-style animation is stunning, and the story earns every bit of its warmth. Great for the whole family, but adults love it just as much.

  2. The Holdovers (2023)

    A grumpy teacher, a stranded student and a snowy winter break

    Set at a 1970s boarding school over the holidays, it's funny, bittersweet and quietly moving. It looks and feels like a film from that era, which makes it a perfect cozy-but-grown-up Christmas watch.

  3. Tokyo Godfathers (2003)

    Three unlikely friends find a baby on Christmas Eve in Tokyo

    Satoshi Kon's anime is a chaotic, big-hearted chain of coincidences across a snowy Tokyo. It's a Christmas movie that most people have never heard of — and one they keep recommending once they've seen it.

  4. Arthur Christmas (2011)

    Santa's clumsy son has one night to deliver a forgotten gift

    A fast, clever comedy from Aardman that imagines Christmas Eve as a high-tech operation. It's full of jokes for every age and ends up being genuinely sweet about family.
