Loved Pride & Prejudice? Watch these 3 next

If Pride & Prejudice (2005) is the movie you rewatch every cold evening, these three period romances share its wit, slow-burn longing and sharp heroines. Two are Jane Austen adaptations, one is a Louisa May Alcott classic told in a fresh way.

  1. Emma. (2020)

    Austen's matchmaker, pastel-perfect and genuinely funny

    Anya Taylor-Joy plays a clever, meddling heroine who is sure she knows everyone's heart better than they do. It's bright, witty and beautifully designed, with a romance that sneaks up on you.

  2. Little Women (2019)

    Four sisters, big dreams and a love story that hurts

    Greta Gerwig's version jumps between past and present, so the March sisters' story feels lively and modern. Warm, emotional and perfect for a winter evening.

  3. Sense and Sensibility (1995)

    Emma Thompson's Austen: restraint, longing, pure payoff

    Two sisters, one ruled by reason and one by feeling, face love and money troubles in Regency England. Emma Thompson wrote the screenplay, and the slow burn is worth every minute.
