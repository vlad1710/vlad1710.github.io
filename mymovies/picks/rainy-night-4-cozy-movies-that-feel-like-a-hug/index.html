Rainy night? 4 cozy movies that feel like a hug

When it's dark by five and raining outside, you want a film that feels like a warm blanket. Here are four feel-good, spoiler-free comfort movies for a cozy November night.

  1. Paddington 2 (2017)

    Pure kindness, marmalade and one of the best-rated films ever

    A warm, funny and beautifully made family film that works just as well for adults. Paddington tries to buy the perfect birthday present and ends up in a very unexpected place. Impossible to watch without smiling.

  2. About Time (2013)

    Gentle time travel used for the small moments that matter

    A young man learns he can revisit moments of his own life, and uses it for love, family and ordinary days. Funny, tender and quietly wise — the perfect blanket-and-tea movie.

  3. Chef (2014)

    A road trip, a food truck and a dad reconnecting with his son

    A chef quits his restaurant job and starts over with a food truck. Light, upbeat and full of mouth-watering cooking scenes — don't watch it hungry.

  4. The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014)

    A candy-coloured caper in a hotel you'll want to live in

    Wes Anderson's playful adventure about a legendary concierge and his loyal lobby boy. Every frame looks like a postcard, and the pace never lets up.
