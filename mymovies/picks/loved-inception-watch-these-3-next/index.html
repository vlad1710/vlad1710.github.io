Loved Inception? Watch these 3 next

If Inception left you replaying scenes in your head, these three films hit the same spot: loops, paradoxes and realities that don't quite add up. All spoiler-free, all best watched knowing as little as possible.

  1. Source Code (2011)

    8 minutes on a train, again and again — until he cracks it

    A soldier keeps waking up in the same eight minutes before a disaster and has to find out who is behind it. Tight, fast and full of the same 'what is real?' energy as Inception, but in a compact 90-minute package.

  2. Predestination (2014)

    A time-travel puzzle that keeps folding in on itself

    Ethan Hawke plays a time-travelling agent chasing one last criminal. It starts as a quiet bar conversation and turns into one of the most twisty time-loop stories on film. Go in knowing as little as possible.

  3. Coherence (2013)

    A dinner party, a comet, and reality starts to crack

    Eight friends, one house, one strange night. Made on a tiny budget, it builds tension out of conversation and small details — perfect if you loved piecing Inception together after the credits.
