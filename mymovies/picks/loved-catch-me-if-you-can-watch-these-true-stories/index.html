Loved Catch Me If You Can? Watch these true stories

Loved Catch Me If You Can for its charming con, real-life twists and "this actually happened?" feeling? These true-story films deliver the same thrill: clever outsiders, impossible plans and stakes that were very real. No spoilers, just reasons to press play.

  1. The Social Network (2010)

    Fast, razor-sharp dialogue about how Facebook really began

    Aaron Sorkin's script and David Fincher's direction turn lawsuits and code into a thriller. Like Catch Me If You Can, it follows a brilliant young man bending the rules — and the people left in his wake.

  2. Moneyball (2011)

    An underdog bets everything on numbers nobody believes in

    Brad Pitt plays a baseball manager who rebuilds a broke team with statistics. You don't need to know anything about baseball — it's a smart, warm story about outthinking the system.

  3. Argo (2012)

    A fake movie used to pull off a real rescue — tense to the end

    The CIA invents a sci-fi film production as cover for a rescue mission. It has the same 'too wild to be true' hook as Catch Me If You Can, plus edge-of-your-seat suspense.

  4. The Big Short (2015)

    Outsiders spot the 2008 crash coming — funny and furious

    A handful of oddballs see the housing collapse before anyone else and bet against the banks. Playful, fourth-wall-breaking style makes complex finance easy and genuinely entertaining.
