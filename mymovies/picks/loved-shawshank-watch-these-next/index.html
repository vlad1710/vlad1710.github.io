Loved Shawshank? Watch these next

If The Shawshank Redemption is the film you'd rewatch any night, here are four classics with the same mix of patience, friendship and quiet defiance. All about people who refuse to give up behind prison walls — spoiler-free.

  1. The Great Escape (1963)

    An all-star POW breakout plan, tense and fun

    Allied prisoners in a German camp plan an ambitious mass escape. It's long but never drags, with a famous cast and a real sense of camaraderie that Shawshank fans will recognise.

  2. Cool Hand Luke (1967)

    Paul Newman's quiet rebel can't be broken

    A charming loner on a Southern chain gang refuses to bend to the rules. Newman is magnetic, and the film is as much about the men watching him as about Luke himself.

  3. Papillon (1973)

    Years of grit and one unbreakable friendship

    Steve McQueen and Dustin Hoffman play convicts sent to a brutal penal colony. A slow-burn survival story built around loyalty and sheer endurance.

  4. Escape from Alcatraz (1979)

    Clint Eastwood vs. the 'escape-proof' prison

    Based on a real 1962 escape attempt, this is a lean, methodical thriller. Little dialogue, lots of tension, and every detail of the plan matters.
