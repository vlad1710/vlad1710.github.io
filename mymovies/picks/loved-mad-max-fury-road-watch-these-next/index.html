Loved Mad Max: Fury Road? Watch these next

Fury Road set a new bar for action: practical stunts, relentless pace and a world you understand without a single info-dump. These three films chase the same adrenaline — no spoilers, just pure momentum for your next movie night.

  1. The Raid (2011)

    One building, wall-to-wall fights, zero breathing room

    A police squad gets trapped inside a tower block run by a crime lord, and the film barely pauses after that. If the nonstop momentum of Fury Road is what hooked you, this is the purest dose of it — practical, brutal and brilliantly choreographed.

  2. Dredd (2012)

    Gritty dystopia, practical action, a hero of few words

    A tough judge and a rookie fight their way up a 200-floor mega-block in a ruined future city. Like Fury Road, it builds a whole world through action instead of exposition, and it has a cult following for good reason.

  3. John Wick (2014)

    Clean, stylish stunt work that made a new action icon

    A retired hitman is pulled back in, and the film turns into a masterclass in long, readable fight scenes. Fans of Fury Road's practical stunts will love how much of the action is real and easy to follow.
