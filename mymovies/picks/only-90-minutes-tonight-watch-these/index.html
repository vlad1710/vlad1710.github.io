Only 90 minutes tonight? Watch these

Got an evening but not three hours? These films all run 90 minutes or less and still feel complete: a breathless thriller, a real-time romance, a one-man drama in a car and a time-loop comedy. Spoiler-free picks for a short movie night.

  1. Run Lola Run (1998)

    20 minutes to save a life. Pure adrenaline, 81 min

    A German thriller that races against the clock with techno, bold style and a clever structure. At 81 minutes it never slows down — perfect when you want energy, not a long commitment.

  2. Before Sunset (2004)

    Two people, one Paris afternoon, real time. 80 min

    Nearly all walking and talking, yet impossible to look away from. It works on its own even if you haven't seen Before Sunrise, and it's one of the most romantic 80 minutes in cinema.

  3. Locke (2013)

    One man, one car, one night. Tom Hardy owns it. 85 min

    The whole film happens during a single drive and a series of phone calls. Tom Hardy carries it alone, and the tension builds quietly but relentlessly — a small film that hits hard.

  4. Palm Springs (2020)

    A time-loop rom-com that's actually funny. 90 min

    Andy Samberg and Cristin Milioti get stuck reliving the same wedding day. Sharp jokes, a surprisingly warm heart, and it wraps up right at the 90-minute mark.
